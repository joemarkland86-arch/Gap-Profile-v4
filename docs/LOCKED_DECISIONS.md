# Locked Decisions

- Four pillars:
  - Capability Belief
  - Risk Tolerance
  - Identity Fit
  - Capacity

- Four archetypes:
  - Capable Doubter
  - Safety Seeker
  - The Unclaimed Self
  - The Overdrawn Self

- Core framing:
  Career development involves many elements, and even when people know the next
  step, taking action can still feel difficult. The Knowing–Doing Gap explains why.

- Tone:
  clear, grounded, structured, psychologically sharp, warm, direct

- Assessment:
  23 questions, radar chart, archetype result, email capture via Formspree
  Current version: V7 (forward-scored items, time anchors, updated band labels)

- Safety Seeker workbook:
  complete (24-page PDF, £9.99)

- Coaching offer and pricing (locked 23 Oct 2026):
  Coaching is client-led. The client brings the agenda; there is no curriculum
  and no advice-giving. Never call the session a "debrief".
  Optional workbook: £9.99, available independently, never a prerequisite.
  GAP Profile Session: £125, one 60-minute coaching conversation.
  Six coaching sessions: £600 total, focus and spacing agreed with the client,
  focus revisited as the work develops, reflection in every session.
  A £125 session counts as the first of six, leaving £475. No credit deadline.
  Two payments of £300 may be offered.
  The package does not appear on the assessment results page; its price can
  appear on a coaching information page.
  Review pricing after the first ten paying clients.

- Results page structure:
  Archetype name → mechanism (short) → reflective question → locked interventions → CTA
  Micro-actions sit behind paywall (workbook or coaching), not on results page

- Results copy evidence standard (locked 23 Oct 2026, evidence sweep v2):
  No customer-facing line may assume that the lowest score is the cause, that
  previous attempts failed, that the person is capable, that the move is right
  for them, or that a step is guaranteed to work. Offer explanations as things
  to check, preferably as questions. Duration and attempt lines describe what
  the person reported and ask questions; they never interpret outcomes.
  Never use: "measures", "predicts", "diagnostic", "the data", brain or threat
  mechanisms, "learned helplessness", "secondary gain", "intervention".
  Citations follow the Brand Strategy v5.2 table. SCARF appears only as coaching
  prompts inside the Safety Seeker workbook. No Baumeister citation.
  Do not announce that the tool is unvalidated; simply make no validity claims.
  No em dashes and no "it's not X, it's Y" constructions in customer copy.
  No-clear-pattern results never push a single workbook.
